PitchProX User Manual

Step-by-step guide to the customer panel · Updated 27 October 2026 · pitchprox.dmango.online

PitchProX helps sales teams organise leads, follow a sales playbook and, soon, make calls with a live AI coach. This manual walks through every feature that works today, click by click. Everything you add is saved and stays after you refresh the page or sign in again.

Websitepitchprox.dmango.online
Best inChrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, on a computer. Phones work, but tables are easier on a big screen.
Need help?Open a support ticket from Settings → Support Ticket (see section 12).

Create a customer account

  1. Go to pitchprox.dmango.online/sign-up.
  2. Enter your full name, email and a password of at least 8 characters, and type the password again to confirm.
  3. Tick the Terms & Conditions box.
  4. Click Create Account. You land on your dashboard, signed in, on the Free Trial plan.

Sign in and sign out

  1. Go to pitchprox.dmango.online/sign-in, enter your email and password, and click Sign In.
  2. To sign out, click Logout at the bottom of the left menu.
Good to knowYou stay signed in for 7 days on each device. "Sign in with Google / Facebook" and "Forgot password" are not connected yet Waiting on client.

1. Dashboard

Left menu → Dashboard · Partly working

The dashboard is your summary. It shows:

  • Conversion Momentum: the share of your leads that reached Qualified or Appointment Set, with the count (for example "3 of 12 leads qualified").
  • Lead Pipeline Overview: how many leads sit in each stage. With no leads yet, it offers an Add a lead button.
  • Prompt Adherence, Trust Gain, Performance Trend and Recent Calls say "No call data yet". They fill in once calling is connected.

2. Leads

Left menu → Leads · Working

Leads are the people you plan to call. Everything here is saved instantly.

Add a lead

  1. Click Add New Lead.
  2. Fill in Full Name and Property Address (both required), up to 6 phone numbers (they format themselves, e.g. (213) 555-1234), email, property address, city, state, zip and lead type (for example Expired Listings or FSBO).
  3. Add any notes (up to 500 characters).
  4. Click Save Lead. You return to the list with your new lead at the top.
Phone numbersYou can type several numbers, but only the first one is saved for now.

Change stage, status or outcome from the list

  1. In the leads table, click the coloured badge in the Stage, Status or Outcome column.
  2. Pick the new value. It saves immediately; if saving fails, the old value comes back and a message explains why.
FieldChoices
StageNew · Attempted · Contacted · In Conversation · Follow-Up · Qualified · Appointment Set · Archived
StatusHot · Warm · Cold · Unknown
OutcomeAppointment Set · Follow-Up Required · Information Requested · Not Interested · Not a Fit · No Answer · Voicemail · Wrong Number · Do Not Contact · Other

Find leads quickly

  • Tabs: All Leads · Hot Leads · Not Connected · Interested / Not Interested.
  • Filters above the table: stage, status, lead type and outcome.
  • Time filter (top right): This Year, This Month, This Week or Today, based on when the lead was last updated.

Open a lead: notes, follow-ups and timeline

  1. Click any lead's row. Its details window opens.
  2. Add a note: click Add Note, type, then Save Note. It appears in the Activity Timeline with your name and the time.
  3. Schedule a follow-up: click Schedule Follow-Up, choose the date, type a time such as 10:00 AM or 14:30, then Schedule Event. The date must be in the future.
  4. The timeline shows the upcoming follow-up, every note (newest first) and when the lead was added.

Edit or delete a lead

  1. Edit: click the pencil icon in the row (or Edit in the details window), change any field, then Update Lead.
  2. Delete: click the bin icon (or Delete in the details window) and confirm. Deleting also removes the lead's notes and cannot be undone.

3. CRM

Left menu → CRM · Working

The CRM tracks prospects, the leads you are actively working, through your pipeline. It works like Leads, with three additions.

  1. Add a prospect: click Add Prospect, fill in the form (name and address are required), choose a stage, status and outcome, then Save Prospect.
  2. Move through the pipeline: click the Stage badge in the table and pick the next stage.
  3. Notes and follow-ups: click a prospect, then use Add Note and Schedule Follow-Up exactly as for leads. The Follow-Up column then shows Scheduled, Due Soon (within 2 days) or Due Now.
  4. Edit: pencil icon or Edit → change fields → Update Prospect.
  5. Import: click Import and choose a .csv or .json file. Each valid row is saved; if some rows are rejected, a message says how many and why.
  6. Export: click Export to download the prospects shown as a CSV file.
CSV import formatThe first row should be headers. Recognised columns: Name, Property Address, Lead Stage, Status, Lead Type, Outcome. Values must match the choices in the Leads table above, e.g. Stage Qualified, Status Warm.

4. Playbooks

Left menu → Playbook · Partly working (buying premium playbooks waits for payments)

A playbook is the sales method your AI coach follows on calls. One playbook is active at a time; new accounts start with PitchProX Standard.

Change the active playbook

  1. Click Change Playbook in the Active Playbook card.
  2. Pick a playbook. Greyed-out ones are premium (not bought yet) or unfinished drafts.
  3. Click Apply Playbook. The card at the top now shows your choice. You can also click Use Playbook on any card.

Create your own playbook

  1. Click Create Playbook on the dotted Custom Playbook card.
  2. Step 1 · Basic Info: name (required), short description, lead types it suits, experience level, your voice and core philosophy, and your main goal.
  3. Step 2 · Your Style: tone, communication style, energy, sentence style, formality and humour.
  4. Step 3 · Conversation Flow: the stages of your call (Opening, Discovery, …). Add, edit, reorder or remove stages.
  5. Step 4 · Objection Handling: the objections you hear and your response framework. Add or edit objections.
  6. Step 5 · Review & Save: check the summary and the completeness score (how much is filled in), then click Create Playbook. It becomes your active playbook.
Save as you goClick Save as Draft at the top at any step. Drafts appear under the Custom tab with a DRAFT badge; click Finish Draft to continue later. Moving between steps never loses what you typed.

Edit or delete your playbook

  1. Open the Custom tab.
  2. Click the pencil to edit. The builder opens with everything filled in; click Save Changes.
  3. Click the bin to delete and confirm. If it was your active playbook, PitchProX Standard becomes active again.

5. AI Training

Left menu → AI Training · Partly working (the AI learns from it once an AI provider is connected)

Upload material that teaches the AI how you sell. There are 7 categories: Sales Bio, Company Info, Scripts & Flows, Objections, Marketing & Listing Presentation, Past Sales Data and Other Resources.

  1. Find the category card and click Upload.
  2. Choose a file: PDF, Word, text, slides, spreadsheets, video (MP4, MOV), audio (MP3, WAV) or images, up to 50 MB.
  3. The file appears in the Training Content table with its type, size and date.
  4. Training Progress shows how many of the 7 categories have material (one category = 14%).
  5. Pause (⏸) keeps a file stored but leaves it out of training; click ▶ to resume. Delete (bin) removes it permanently.
PrivacyDo not upload sensitive client information. Files are stored privately and only your company's accounts can see them.

6. Calibration

Left menu → Calibration · Partly working (scores are samples until AI is connected)

  1. Allow microphone access when your browser asks.
  2. Follow the on-screen rounds: listen, then read the suggested response aloud while recording.
  3. Click End Calibration when done. A message confirms the session was saved and how many sessions you have completed.
The score screen after calibration shows sample numbers until the AI is connected.

7. Profile & password

Left menu → Settings → Profile · Partly working

  1. Change your name: edit the name field and click Save Changes. The new name shows in the left menu immediately.
  2. Change your password: under Change Password, enter your current password and the new one twice (8+ characters), then save.
SecurityChanging your password signs you out on every other device, in case someone else knew the old one. The language and "Delete Your Account" options are not active yet.

8. Preferences & notifications

Settings → Preferences / Notifications · Working

  1. Preferences: set the font size and your default playbook (the same choice as the Playbook page), then click Save Changes.
  2. Notifications: choose how you get in-app notifications and AI insight alerts, then save.

9. Security

Settings → Security · Partly working (2-step login coming later)

See and remove signed-in devices

  1. Device Management lists every device signed in to your account: device type, browser, IP address and when it was last active. "This Device" is the one you are using.
  2. Click the ⋮ menu on a device:
    • Device Info: when it signed in.
    • Refresh Session: keep it signed in for another 7 days.
    • Remove Device: sign that device out immediately.
  3. Click Log out of all other devices to sign out everywhere except here.

Check sign-in history

Login Activity shows your last 20 sign-in attempts with date, time, device, browser, IP and Success or Failed. An unknown device or many Failed rows means someone may be trying your password: change it (section 7) and remove unknown devices.

11. Send Mail

Settings → Send Mail · Partly working (delivery waits for an email service)

  1. Optional: click a template under Quick templates to pre-fill the subject and message.
  2. Enter the recipient's email. Click CC or BCC to add more addresses, separated by commas.
  3. Write the subject and message, then click Send email.
  4. The email appears under recent emails with the status Waiting. Click it to see the full message.
Emails are not delivered yetMessages are saved with a "Waiting" status and will be sent once an email service is connected. The "AI draft" button inserts sample text for now.

12. Support tickets

Settings → Support Ticket · Working

Open a ticket

  1. Under Create a new ticket, choose a category (Technical Support, Billing & Plans, Account Access or Feature Request) and a priority (Low, Medium, High or Urgent).
  2. Write a short subject and describe the problem.
  3. Optional: attach a screenshot or file (images, PDF, documents or logs, up to 10 MB).
  4. Click Create Ticket. A message shows its number, e.g. #TKT-1001, and it appears in Your tickets.

Follow up on a ticket

  1. Click a ticket in Your tickets or Recent support tickets (or View all tickets). The conversation opens on the right.
  2. Type in the reply box and click Send Reply. Use the arrows at the top to move between tickets.
  3. Click the eye or download icon on an attachment to open it. The Attachments tab lists every file on the ticket.
Statuses: Open → In progress → Resolved / Closed. Replying to a resolved or closed ticket opens it again. Replies from the PitchProX team arrive with the Super Admin panel.

Creating staff accounts

Super admins, sales reps and customer team members are created on the server by your developer. There is no sign-up page for them, so nobody can make themselves an admin. Your developer runs one command per account in the project folder:

# Super admin npm run create-user -- --email you@company.com --name "Your Name" --password "a-strong-password" --role super_admin # Sales rep npm run create-user -- --email rep@company.com --name "Rep Name" --password "a-strong-password" --role sales_rep # Team member of an existing customer (use that customer's sign-up email) npm run create-user -- --email staff@customer.com --name "Staff Name" --password "a-strong-password" --role customer_member --company-of owner@customer.com

Use strong passwords of at least 8 characters, and share them privately, never in a document or group chat.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"Invalid email or password"Check for typos and Caps Lock. Password reset by email isn't available yet, so ask the PitchProX team to reset it.
I'm sent back to the sign-in pageYour session ended (after 7 days, a password change, or "Log out of all other devices"). Sign in again. Always use the https:// address.
A page opens a different dashboard than I expectedEach account only opens its own area. A customer account can't open the Super Admin or Sales Rep pages.
Upload failsCheck the file type and size: 50 MB for AI Training, 10 MB for ticket attachments.
An email I "sent" never arrivedExpected for now: emails stay Waiting until an email service is connected.
Numbers on a page look like examplesCheck the label at the bottom of the screen. Preview pages still show sample data.
Something elseOpen a support ticket (section 12) with a screenshot.
